# Privacy Policy - AeroAge ChronOrbit

**Last updated:** October 2026

**AeroAge ChronOrbit** ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
AeroAge ChronOrbit is an offline-first utility application. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information (PII) on external servers. All input data, including birth dates, target dates, and saved reminders, is processed locally on your device.

## 2. Data Storage
Any data saved within the application (such as saved birthday reminders) is stored locally on your mobile device storage and is not accessible by us or any third parties.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The app does not integrate with any third-party analytics or data-tracking SDKs that monitor personal behavior.

## 4. Permissions
The app requires basic system permissions necessary for running standard app functions. It does not request access to contacts, location, gallery, or camera.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13 specifically, but because we collect no personal information, the app is safe for users of all age groups.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page.
